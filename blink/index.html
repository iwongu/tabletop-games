How to Play Blink

The Rule: Play cards from your 3-card hand onto either of the 2 center piles by matching Color, Shape, OR Count!
  • Instant 1-Tap Play: Tap any card in your hand. If it matches only one center pile, it drops there automatically! If it matches both piles, tap your preferred center pile (or tap the card again for Left).
  • No Moves? Tap Flip Piles in the middle to deal fresh center cards.
  • Sprint: Empty your 30-card deck as fast as possible.
  • vs Bot: Real-time race against an AI opponent sharing the same 2 center piles!
  • 2P Duel: Lay the tablet flat between two players—first to empty all 29 cards wins!
