How to Play

Guide the matching-color robot onto the pulsing target square in as few moves as possible.

  • Slide until stopped: Robots never stop mid-floor — they glide straight until hitting a wall or another robot.
  • Teamwork: Move other robots first to act as temporary bumpers!
  • Controls: Tap a robot (or its color chip below) then tap any glowing landing dot, or simply swipe a robot in any direction.
  • Par: Every puzzle is solved by an exact BFS engine so Par is the true shortest path.
