How to Play Swish

The Goal: Pick 2 or more transparent cards that can be flipped or rotated 180° and stacked so every Ball lands inside a Hoop of the exact same color—with nothing left over!
  • Each card is a transparent 3×4 grid with 1 solid Ball and 1 hollow Hoop.
  • Cards may be flipped horizontally, flipped vertically, or rotated 180° (never rotated 90°).
  • Tap 2 cards (or 3–4 cards for bigger combos!). As soon as your selected cards form a valid Swish, you'll see them snap together in their exact stacked solution!
