How to Play Spot It!

The Magic Rule: Any two cards share exactly ONE matching symbol — never zero, never two!

Symbols vary dramatically in size and rotation. Tap the matching symbol on either card as fast as you can.

  • Sprint: Clear the full deck in the fastest time.
  • 60s Blitz: Score as many matches as possible in 60 seconds (streaks multiply points; misses cost 2s).
  • Zen: Untimed relaxed spotting with hints.
  • 2P Duel: Lay the tablet flat between two players and tap the match on your own card!
